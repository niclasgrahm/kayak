# kayak contributor licence agreement

*Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor Licence
Agreement v2.0. It is not legal advice, and it has not been reviewed by a
lawyer — if you are contributing on behalf of an employer, have someone who
knows your employment agreement read it first.*

To keep kayak's licensing decisions possible, contributions need this agreement.
Read it, then see [signing](#signing) at the end.

**You keep the copyright in everything you write.** This is a licence you grant,
not an assignment. You can go on using your own contribution however you like,
including in other projects.

## 1. definitions

**"You"** means the copyright owner entering into this agreement — you, or the
legal entity you are authorised to act for.

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship you intentionally submit for
inclusion in kayak, in any form, including any modification to existing work.
"Submitted" means sent to the project by any means — a pull request, a patch, an
issue, electronic mail — except anything you clearly mark **"Not a
Contribution."**

**"The maintainer"** means Niclas Grahm, and any successor to whom the copyright
in kayak is transferred.

maintainer, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free,
irrevocable copyright licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of,

**This includes the right to distribute your Contribution under licence terms of
the maintainer's choosing, including terms different from the ones kayak carries
today.** That is what this agreement is for: it means kayak can be offered under
a commercial licence alongside the AGPL, and can be relicensed in future,
without needing every past contributor's agreement.

If you submit work that is not your original creation, submit it **separately**
from any Contribution, identifying its source and any licence or restriction
that applies to it, and mark it clearly **"Submitted on behalf of a third party:
[name]"**.

If anything in your representations later becomes inaccurate, tell the
maintainer.

## signing

There is no bot wired up yet, because there have been no external contributions
yet. Until there is one, sign by adding this line to your pull request
description, with your own name and the date:

```
I have read the CLA in cla.md and I agree to it.
Signed: <your full name>, <date>, GitHub: @<your username>
```

A pull request without it can be reviewed and discussed, but cannot be merged.
